# No-patent pledge

The BotVow contributors who sign off this repository pledge **not to seek patents** on the BotVow
format, the botvow/0.1 specification, or the reference tools in this repository, and not to assert
any patent against anyone who implements, uses or improves them.

BotVow is meant to be an open, free building block for the black box of Bots. Anyone may implement
it, in any language and on any platform, without asking for permission and without paying.

This pledge does not grant rights to any third-party patent. We are aware of a third-party
Internet-Draft in a neighbouring area whose author has declared a pending patent application;
implementers should take their own legal advice.

---

**Compromisso de não patentear (PT).** Os contribuidores do BotVow comprometem-se a **não pedir
patentes** sobre o formato BotVow, a especificação botvow/0.1 ou as ferramentas deste repositório,
e a não invocar patentes contra quem os implemente, use ou melhore.
